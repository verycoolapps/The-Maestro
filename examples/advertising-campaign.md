# Advertising Campaign Example

### Case Study: ZERON "Nothing Left Behind" Campaign
### PT. Agra Karya Digital (VeryCoolApps) © 2026

---

> **Context:** ZERON is a zero-waste consumer electronics brand launching its flagship product — the ZERON X — a smartphone built from 100% recycled materials, with a fully circular take-back program, and a 10-year software support commitment. They need an integrated campaign to launch the product in Southeast Asian urban markets. The campaign must cut through in a category where "sustainability" messaging has become so common it has become almost meaningless.

---

## CAMPAIGN BRIEF

**Brand:** ZERON
**Product:** ZERON X Smartphone
**Campaign Objective:** Launch the ZERON X in Jakarta, Singapore, and Bangkok — establishing ZERON as the most intellectually honest electronics brand for people who know the actual environmental cost of consumer electronics.
**Timeline:** 8-week launch campaign
**Budget:** USD 1.2M (across all channels)

**Campaign business objectives:**
1. Drive 15,000 pre-orders in first 30 days (primary)
2. Achieve 70%+ aided brand awareness in target segment within 60 days (secondary)
3. Generate earned media coverage in at least 5 tier-1 publications per market (secondary)

## AUDIENCE INSIGHT — DEEP PSYCHOGRAPHIC PROFILE

**Who they are:**
Urban professionals, 24–38, in Southeast Asian cities. Economically comfortable. Educated, often internationally. Tech-aware — they understand how devices work, understand the supply chain reality of consumer electronics, understand what "sustainability" claims usually mean (very little).

**What they know that most people don't:**
They know that every smartphone requires lithium mining, cobalt extraction, rare earth processing — and that the human and environmental cost of these processes is enormous. They know that "recycled packaging" claims are largely irrelevant when the device itself is fundamentally a disposable object. They know that most sustainability marketing from electronics brands is greenwashing.

**The specific tension they live in:**
They need smartphones. They cannot opt out of the category. They have made peace with this — but they carry a quiet discomfort about it. They've never found a brand that acknowledged this discomfort honestly, let alone addressed it.

**What they're not:**
They are not "eco-warriors" who will sacrifice performance for principle. They want a phone that is excellent AND honest. They will not buy a worse phone because it's greener. They need ZERON to be both.

**Their relationship to sustainability marketing:**
Skeptical. Very skeptical. The word "sustainable" has been destroyed by misuse. They will not respond to any campaign that uses it without evidence. They will immediately distrust any brand that claims to be sustainable without specific, verifiable proof.

**The aha insight:**
"I know the cost of the phone in my pocket. I'd prefer not to know. That discomfort is the thing nobody in this category has ever addressed directly — because addressing it honestly would implicate every player in the category, including themselves. ZERON is the only brand that can address it, because they're the only brand doing something real about it."

---

## STRATEGIC PLATFORM

**The Insight:**
Smartphone users in this segment know the environmental cost of their devices — and have been trained by the entire industry to look away. Every sustainability campaign in electronics has asked them to focus on what brands are doing right, rather than the magnitude of what the entire category is doing wrong.

**The Tension:**
You need a phone. You know what a phone costs the world. You can't not know, once you know. And nobody in this category has ever respected you enough to look you in the eye about it.

**ZERON's Role:**
The first brand in consumer electronics to tell the truth about the category, then demonstrate — specifically and verifiably — what it means to actually do something about it.

**The Creative Mandate:**
No greenwashing language. No aspirational environmental imagery (forests, clean water, smiling children). No percentage claims without full context. No hedging. Tell the truth about the problem, then tell the truth about the solution — and let the audience decide if it's enough.

---

## THE BIG IDEA

**"Nothing Left Behind"**

*The idea in full:* Every phone eventually becomes waste. The question isn't whether — it's what happens to it, and to everything that made it. ZERON X leaves nothing behind — in the materials it's made from, in the software it supports for 10 years, in the take-back program that processes every unit into the next generation. "Nothing Left Behind" is simultaneously an environmental promise and an expression of commitment to the customer.

**The aha insight that powers it:**
"Left behind" has two meanings: environmental waste (the material left behind in landfills and mining tailings) and the feeling of being abandoned (the customer who bought last year's flagship, only to find it unsupported 2 years later). Both meanings are true for the target audience. Both meanings ZERON X addresses. The idea works on both levels simultaneously — which is what a great Big Idea does.

## THREE CAMPAIGN TERRITORIES

---

### TERRITORY A — "THE RECKONING"
*The territory of radical transparency*

**Campaign concept:** ZERON X launches with the most honest advertising any electronics brand has ever created — a campaign that openly states the environmental cost of a standard smartphone, then demonstrates specifically what ZERON X does differently.

**Flagship execution (60-second film):**
Opens with a beautiful shot of a standard flagship smartphone on a minimal surface. The narration begins, quietly, with a voice that is calm and specific — not dramatic:

*"This phone required approximately 70 different minerals to build. Its lithium came from Chile's Atacama Desert, where mining operations have reduced water availability for local communities by an estimated 65%. Its cobalt — a component of its battery — may have been extracted in conditions that have been documented as unsafe. Its case will outlast the software support you'll receive for it by approximately eight years. It is an extraordinary piece of engineering. It is also the product of an extractive system that every buyer is participating in."*

Pause.

*"You are holding a ZERON X. 100% recycled metals. Fully circular take-back program. 10-year software support commitment. You should know what it still costs. We're working on that too."*

No music. No aspirational imagery. Just the truth, delivered by a brand that has earned the right to deliver it because they've done the work first.

**Campaign line:** "Nothing Left Behind."

**Tagline options:**
1. "Nothing Left Behind." (Primary)
2. "The truth is the most sustainable material we have." (Earned media / manifesto)
3. "Ten years, not two." (Performance advertising emphasis)

### TERRITORY B — "THE CALCULATION"
*The territory of verifiable specificity*

**Campaign concept:** Every piece of ZERON X communication is built around specific, verifiable numbers — not percentages, not "up to" claims, not "approximately." Exact numbers, with sources cited. The campaign demonstrates honesty through mathematical precision.

**Flagship execution:**
A campaign that shows the actual numbers: how many grams of recycled material. How many years of software support. How many devices have been processed through the take-back program. How many ZERON X units have been pre-ordered since launch. Updated in real time where possible.

**Campaign line:** "The numbers don't lie."

## THE RECOMMENDATION: TERRITORY A — "THE RECKONING"

Territory A scores 95/100 — genuinely world-class creative potential. It is the only direction that fully honors the brand's specific positioning advantage: the first electronics brand to tell the complete truth. Territory B is too rational — it doesn't create the emotional breakthrough the campaign needs. Territory C is beautiful but doesn't leverage the most powerful asset: ZERON's unique credibility to address the category's systemic problem directly.

Territory C's visual idea (the material chain between generations) should be incorporated as the product demonstration element within Territory A's executions.

## INTEGRATED CHANNEL STRATEGY

### Television / SVOD (40% of budget)
**Primary execution:** 60-second manifesto film (the full Reckoning narration)
**Supporting:** 30-second product story, 15-second pre-order CTA

**Direction:** No aspirational lifestyle footage. Product on clean surface. Voice-over. The minimalism is the message. Shot in single-take style — the camera never moves while the narrator speaks.

---

### Digital / Online Video (25% of budget)
**YouTube:** 60-second manifesto (non-skippable pre-roll on tech, news, environmental content)
**Programmatic video:** 15-second product spec cut
**Social video:** Platform-specific adaptations

**TikTok strategy:** THE RECKONING format adapted for native TikTok: "Things your phone company doesn't want you to know" — but the actual facts, stated without sensationalism. ZERON is the brand that tells you the truth, then shows what they're doing about it. Partner with tech commentators who already speak this truth to their audiences.

---

### Out-of-Home (15% of budget)
**Primary markets:** Jakarta (Sudirman corridor, Kuningan), Singapore (Orchard, CBD), Bangkok (Sukhumvit, Silom)

**OOH execution:**
Large format. Black and white. Data-first.

```
THE DEVICE IN YOUR POCKET 
WILL BE UNSUPPORTED IN 2 YEARS.
WILL BE IN A LANDFILL IN 5.

ZERON X. 10 YEARS. NOTHING LEFT BEHIND.
```

No photography. No product image (until secondary placements). Just the truth, in a typeface large enough to read from a vehicle.

---

### Social Media (10% of budget)
**Instagram:** Precision documentation of ZERON X's material composition — the actual photographs of recycled materials entering the production process. The editorial photography of industrial truth.

**X (Twitter):** Real-time transparency — the take-back program numbers, updated weekly. The honesty performance, made public.

**LinkedIn:** The B2B angle: corporate device programs, sustainability reporting alignment, 10-year support reducing total cost of ownership.

---

### Experiential (5% of budget)
**The Reckoning Installation:** A physical installation in one high-traffic urban location per market. A transparent display case containing exactly the quantity of minerals and materials required to build a standard smartphone, next to the ZERON X. Labeled. Verifiable. QR codes linking to source citations.

**Media strategy:** Earned media hook — no paid promotion of the installation. It earns its own coverage, or it isn't honest enough.

---

### PR / Earned Media (5% of budget)
**The pitch:** An electronics brand advertising the environmental cost of electronics — including cost ZERON itself hasn't solved yet. This is genuinely newsworthy because no brand has done this.

**Target publications:** Wired, TechCrunch, Guardian, Kompas Tekno, TechinAsia, regional sustainability-focused media.

**The story:** ZERON is not claiming to have solved the problem. They are claiming to have done more than anyone else, and to tell the truth about how much more there is to do. This is the story.

---

## KEY VISUAL DIRECTION

**Primary key visual:** ZERON X on a neutral surface. Black background. Single overhead light source creating precise shadows. No retouching of material texture — the recycled composition of the phone's casing is visible at close inspection.

**Copy treatment:** White, uppercase, minimal. The numbers are displayed in a larger point size than the brand name. The specifics outrank the brand.

**What is not in the key visual:** No hands (lifestyle). No bokeh (aspiration). No environmental imagery. No people. The product, the truth, and nothing else.

---

## COPY DIRECTION

**Headline philosophy:** State the most uncomfortable truth first. ZERON has the credibility to say what competitors cannot — lead with that.

**Strong headline examples:**

For OOH (data-first):
> "Your last 3 phones are in a landfill.
> Your ZERON X won't be."

For digital (performance):
> "10 years of support.
> Because you shouldn't need a new phone in 2 years."

For manifesto (brand):
> "We didn't solve the problem.
> We did more than anyone else, and we're not pretending otherwise.
> ZERON X."

**Body copy rhythm:** Short sentences. Specific claims. No hedging language. If a claim cannot be verified with a source citation, do not make it.
